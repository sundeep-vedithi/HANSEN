# HANSEN data licence and source attribution

The archived HANSEN data in
`src/hansen_database/data/hansen_snapshot.json.gz.b64.part-*` and the generated
TSV files are provided under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
where the conditions of the original scientific resources permit this use.

Users should cite the versioned HANSEN repository release and the relevant
source databases and computational methods.

The archive contains selected identifiers, annotations and calculated values
from UniProt, the Protein Data Bank Chemical Component Dictionary, Gene
Ontology and the computational methods identified in the HANSEN documentation.
Each source remains subject to its own licence, attribution requirements and
conditions of use. Where a source imposes a more restrictive condition, that
condition applies to the corresponding HANSEN value.

HANSEN is provided for scientific research and education. Some integrated
methods restrict commercial use of their outputs. Users must examine the
current HANSEN terms and the terms of each relevant method before HANSEN data
are redistributed or used for commercial purposes.

This repository does not distribute structure coordinates, model parameters
or complete copies of the source databases.
